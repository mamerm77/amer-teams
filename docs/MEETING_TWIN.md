# Meeting digital twin

Chat menu → **Meeting twin**: everything a recurring meeting has produced, built **on this device** from the chat's own end-to-end encrypted history (about the last 1,600 events).

- **Counts:** meetings with minutes, decisions, open actions, overdue actions.
- **Every meeting:** all minutes (online, in-person and post-incident reviews) by date.
- **Decisions** from the ledger, and **open actions** with owner and due date, overdue first.
- **Keeps coming back:** terms that appear in three or more sets of minutes (for example, "Nokia spares ×5").
- **Ask the meeting's history:** a question answered from all of the above only, citing the meeting date for each point, through the encrypted-AI router (labels honoured).

## Open gaps
- History reaches back as far as the device can load (about 1,600 events); older meetings need search.
- Recurring topics are found by term counting, not meaning.
- No "simulate the next meeting" prediction yet: the twin reflects what happened.
