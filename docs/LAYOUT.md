# Layout (modelled on Microsoft Teams and Slack)

**App bar (left):** Activity, Chat, Teams, Calendar, Calls, Amer, Apps. This is the same short set Teams keeps visible since its 2026 app-bar simplification. On phones there are five tabs (Activity, Chat, Teams, Calendar, More), as in the Teams and Slack mobile apps.

**Top bar:** the product name; **Search messages, files and people** in the middle (opens Search everywhere); your profile on the right (Notifications, Security, Accessibility, build number, Sign out).

**Chat:** on a computer, the chat list and the open chat sit side by side. On a phone the chat opens full screen.

**Apps:** every other feature, grouped (Your work · Meetings and calls · Amer and agents · Operations · Administration · Settings). Each has one line saying what it does, and you can search it. Features that live inside a chat or a meeting are listed with where to find them ("Chat → menu (⋮) or type /", "Meeting → More").

**Meeting bar:** microphone, camera and share sit together in the middle, with raise hand, reactions and Ask Amer next to them. Captions, Amer minutes, background blur, low light and switch camera are under **More**. **Leave** is red, on the far right, away from the other controls (as in Teams' 2026 meeting redesign). Everything else (recording, guest link, dial-in, Q&A, translated captions, network) stays in the meeting's top-right menu.
