# In-person meeting notes

Calendar → **In-person notes**: for a meeting in a room, not a call.

1. Choose the chat for the minutes and, if you like, type the agenda.
2. **Start** and put the device on the table. Speech is transcribed on the device (dictation mode, restarted automatically after pauses). No audio is kept.
3. Tap **Now speaking** to mark who is talking. Add names as they appear in the chat, so actions get the right owner.
4. **Finish and write minutes**: Amer writes the minutes (following the agenda when given) and posts them to the chat. Decisions become proposals to confirm in the decision ledger, and actions become tasks with owners and due dates. This is the same follow-up as for online meetings, through the encrypted-AI router.

## Open gaps
- No automatic speaker separation (diarisation): speakers are marked by hand.
- Quality depends on one device's microphone and the speech engine's language (Arabic and English work best).
