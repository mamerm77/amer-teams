# Video recap with chapters

When a recording made with consent (RECORDINGS.md) is shared, Amer adds a **recap** in that recording's thread: a three-sentence summary and 3–10 chapters (`mm:ss  title`) at the topic changes.

The recap is built from the meeting's captions during the recording, with times measured from the recording's start. Captions come from people's own devices while Amer is on, or from your own captions. It goes through the encrypted-AI router (labels honoured), and chapter times not in `mm:ss` form are dropped.

## Open gaps
- Without captions during the recording there is no recap (nothing to chapter).
- Chapters are text; tapping one doesn't yet seek the video (the app has no built-in video player; the file opens in the system player).
- No highlight clips.
